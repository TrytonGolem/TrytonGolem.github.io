Photography

I've been learning photography since 2023. It first started off by simply finding the right angles and proper lighting conditions while simply using my phone.

Later I decided to use an old digital camera Samsung WB700 to get a proper camera feel. Though I've only used it using auto mode.

In 2025 I got a proper mirrorless camera ZV-E10 with 16-50mm F3.5-5.6 and 50mm F1.8 lenses. At first I also used it in auto mode only. Eventually I started playing around with half-manual modes and ultimately I started it using manually fully.
